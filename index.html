FlowCus - Free Pomodoro Timer App for Focus and Productivity

FlowCus is a free, beautiful Pomodoro timer application designed to help you stay focused and boost your productivity. Using the proven Pomodoro Technique, FlowCus offers 25 and 50 minute focus sessions followed by 5 or 15 minute breaks.

Features of FlowCus Pomodoro Timer

  • 25 minute and 50 minute focus sessions
  • 5 minute short breaks and 15 minute long breaks
  • Automatic break reminders after 4 focus cycles
  • Beautiful liquid glass UI design
  • Multiple color themes: Dark, Light, Midnight, Sunset, Ocean
  • Customizable accent colors
  • Sound notifications when timer completes
  • Works offline - no internet required
  • No account or signup needed
  • Completely free with no ads

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo. It uses a timer to break work into intervals, traditionally 25 minutes in length, separated by short breaks. FlowCus implements this technique with customizable focus and break durations.

How to Use FlowCus

Select your focus duration (25 or 50 minutes), click Start, and begin your focused work session. When the timer ends, take a short 5-minute break. After completing 4 focus sessions, enjoy a longer 15-minute break. FlowCus tracks your cycles automatically.
